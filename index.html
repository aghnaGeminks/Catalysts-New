Questions

Everything you need to know before starting a custom Minecraft build with us.

What kind of Minecraft builds do you create?

We create lobbies, spawn hubs, cities, dungeons, arenas, landmarks, and custom worlds tailored to your server.

Can I request a custom style or theme?

Yes. You can share references, a mood, a story, or a gameplay goal, and we will shape the design around it.

How long does a project usually take?

The timeline depends on the scale and detail of the build. We provide a clear schedule after reviewing your brief.

How do I start a project?

Send us your idea, references, expected size, and deadline. We will follow up with the next steps and a concept direction.
